An organisation is one company: its legal name, company number and VAT number appear on its
invoices. Someone who runs two companies has two organisations under one login and switches
between them; each keeps its own sites, customers and orders.

Your login belongs to you, not to a mailbox: use a personal address to sign in, and keep shared
addresses like info@ or sales@ for receiving mail.
