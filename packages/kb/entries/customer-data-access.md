**What the Portal holds:** account and organisation names, members, plan and billing status,
and per server: name, version, health, and counts (organisations, sites, seats, storage).
It does **not** hold customers' orders, products, visitors or their customers' personal data.

**Sharing:** only with a verified member of that customer's account. Never confirm to a third
party that someone is a customer.

**Server addresses and access:** server IPs and shell access are staff-only and must not appear
in any public or customer-facing answer. Access to a customer's server requires their explicit,
time-limited permission and is logged.
