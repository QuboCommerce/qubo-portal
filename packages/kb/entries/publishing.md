Qubo isn't an app store: you can publish a site the moment it's ready, even a quick one for a
weekend. Publishing confirms which organisation owns the site (see `site-transfer`).

Published sites are scanned automatically for phishing, malware and abuse. A person only looks at
a site when the scan flags it. Flagged sites can be taken offline on Qubo cloud while we review;
on your own server, you remain responsible for what you host.
