You don't need a Portal account to run Qubo. Without one, a server runs on {{plans.free.name}}
({{plans.free.sites}} site). Linking a Portal account brings your plan's limits, update notices
and an overview of your servers.

Your site never depends on the Portal being online. The licence is renewed every
{{licence.heartbeat}} and stays valid for {{licence.validity}} (+ {{licence.grace}} grace) without contact.
