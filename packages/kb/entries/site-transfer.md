**Draft sites** (never published) can move to another organisation at any time, free of charge.

**Published sites stay where they are.** Orders, invoices, customers and their history are tied to
the organisation (the legal entity) that published the site, so moving it would rewrite who sold
what to whom. That's why publishing asks you to confirm the organisation first.

**Special cases** (a company takeover, a mistake at launch) can be handled by hand through
{{services.migration.name}}: {{services.migration.price}} per site, after a review. Included in:
{{services.migration.includedIn}}. We may decline a migration that would break legal records.
